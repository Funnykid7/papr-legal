Papr Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

Papr is a document scanner for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: your documents stay on your phone. Papr has no accounts, no ads, and no servers of its own.

Your documents

  • Scans, page images, PDFs, OCR text, signatures, highlights, and notes are stored only in Papr's private storage on your device.
  • Papr never uploads your documents anywhere. A document leaves your phone only when you choose to share or export it, and then it goes only where you send it, through Android's share sheet.
  • Uninstalling Papr, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, deletes all of your documents permanently.

Scanning and text recognition

Papr uses Google's ML Kit document scanner and text recognition, which run inside Google Play services on your device. Your page images and their text are not sent to Google or anyone else for recognition.

To keep these features working, Google Play services may send Google limited technical information about how they run, such as device model and Android version, app package name and version, performance and error metrics, and a per-installation identifier that isn't meant to identify you or your device. Google describes this in ML Kit's data disclosure. It does not include your documents or their contents.

Papr does not ask for camera permission. The scanner runs as part of Google Play services, which asks for camera access itself.

Purchases

Papr Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play. Papr never sees your payment details. It only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Papr Pro, so it can turn Pro features on, or off again if the purchase is refunded. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Settings and usage counts

Papr keeps your settings (theme, default export format, and similar choices), whether Pro is unlocked, and how many limited features you've used this month, stored on your device. If you have Android backup turned on, these settings, and nothing else from Papr, may be included in your Google account's device backup, so they come back when you set up a new phone. Your documents are never part of that cloud backup. A direct phone-to-phone transfer, when you set up a new phone with a cable or Wi-Fi transfer, may copy your documents across too.

What Papr does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or crash reporting of its own.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.

Children

Papr is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13.
